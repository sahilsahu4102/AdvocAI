# Surface brief: Animal Advocacy Research (src/app/page.tsx)

Scope and mode: Operate. One route. An advocacy researcher asks a question, waits about 20 seconds, then reads, copies and checks a cited brief against the pages behind it.

Audience, job, constraints: see PRODUCT.md. Content is real only: the workflow's response shape, nothing invented. Region steers the model, it does not filter the search. No attach button, no Verified badge, no persisted history.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every line of the brief is a pinned specimen with its data label directly beneath it: source letter in that page's own colour, host, short page title. A line with no label is never shown. The three pages read are the three label colours, so every claim visibly hangs from a page and the researcher sees which pages they have opened. Refuses the category default: a chat bubble or one undifferentiated emerald card per section with a faint host link at the end of the sentence.

OWN-WORLD: Natural-history collection drawer. Cabinet green (#25362C) shell bar, grey-green foam ground (#E3E8DF), white label cards (#FBFBF7), ink (#1B1F1B), three source colours blue #1F5FA8, amber #8A5D00, plum #7A3E8A, each always paired with its letter A, B or C. Red is reserved for failure only. Flat: no shadow, blur or gradient; 1.5px ink rules, square label corners. Recognisable with content removed by the label card: a square letter tab filled in the source colour (never a coloured border-left), the host in mono, a short page title, an excerpt that unfolds in place. Face: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next with its Mono for hosts and timers.

STORY: The researcher understands that nothing on screen is uncited and which page each line hangs from; believes it because the page itself is the evidence (label, host, title, snippet unfolding in place); and acts by opening a source, copying a section with its links, or running a follow-up.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Cabinet-green bar: name left, History button right. Below, centred on the foam ground, one white question card at a 44rem measure: the query textarea, a labelled Focus region select with a one-line note that it steers the analysis, and the Research button (primary, ink); six starter topics beneath as ordinary buttons. A strip under it states what a brief contains (cited insights, organisations, trends, actions; three pages read) without claims of speed or accuracy. After a run: the same card compacts and the brief fills the column: question and date, a source key (A, B, C), then four sections, then Explore more.

FORM: The Specimen Drawer, assigned index 5 of my ordered list of seven grounded candidates; seed key c7f69861.
